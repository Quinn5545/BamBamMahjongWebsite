A Living Tradition

Dragon Tile Mahjong has been bringing the ancient art of mahjong to players of all skill levels since 2010. Whether you're a curious newcomer or a seasoned competitor, we offer an authentic, welcoming experience rooted in the game's rich cultural heritage.

We host weekly game nights, private lessons, and regional tournaments — plus a carefully curated shop featuring hand-crafted tile sets, bamboo racks, and accessories sourced directly from Guangdong.
