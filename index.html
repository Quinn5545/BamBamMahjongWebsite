Our Story

A Welcoming Place
to Play & Learn

At Bam Bam Mahjong, we believe Mahjong is more than a game—it’s a way to bring people together. Our passion is creating welcoming, elevated experiences where friends gather, new connections are made, and everyone feels confident at the table. From beginner lessons and private parties to country clubs, corporate events, and leagues, we make learning Mahjong fun, social, and memorable.

We operate in Northern California Bay area & Palm Springs area.
